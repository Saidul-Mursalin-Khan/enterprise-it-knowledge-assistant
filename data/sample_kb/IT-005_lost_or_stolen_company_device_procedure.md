# Lost or Stolen Company Device Procedure

**Document ID:** IT-005  
**Owner:** IT Security  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Demo policy — fictional company data

## Immediate reporting
Immediately report a lost or stolen company laptop to both the IT Service Desk and the employee's manager.

## Information to provide
Provide the device asset tag if available, the last known location, and the approximate time the device was last seen. Do not delay reporting if the asset tag is unavailable.

## IT response
IT will initiate applicable device lock or wipe procedures and coordinate with Security.

## Employee actions
Do not attempt to remotely recover the device yourself. Do not confront a suspected thief. If the device may have been stolen, follow local safety guidance and cooperate with IT and Security.
