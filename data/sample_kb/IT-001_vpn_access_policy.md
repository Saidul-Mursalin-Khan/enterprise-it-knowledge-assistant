# VPN Access Policy

**Document ID:** IT-001  
**Owner:** IT Support  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Demo policy — fictional company data

## Policy
Employees working outside the office must connect to the company VPN before accessing internal systems. The approved VPN client is NovaSecure VPN, available from the company Software Portal.

## Connection procedure
1. Install NovaSecure VPN from the company Software Portal.
2. Sign in using the employee's company email and password.
3. Complete multi-factor authentication (MFA).
4. Select the closest available corporate gateway.
5. Confirm the VPN shows a connected status before opening internal systems.

## Troubleshooting
If the VPN fails, confirm internet connectivity, restart the VPN client, and try another corporate gateway. Do not disable endpoint protection or firewall settings to make the VPN work. If the issue continues, create an IT Service Desk ticket and attach a screenshot of the error.

## Escalation
Contact the IT Service Desk if the employee cannot connect after the listed troubleshooting steps. Never send passwords or MFA codes in the ticket.
