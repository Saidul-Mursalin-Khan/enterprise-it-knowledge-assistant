# Email and Phishing Response Guide

**Document ID:** IT-006  
**Owner:** IT Security  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Demo policy — fictional company data

## Warning signs
Treat unexpected requests for passwords, MFA codes, urgent payments, gift cards, confidential files, or unusual login verification as suspicious. Check the sender address carefully and be cautious with unexpected attachments or links.

## What to do
Do not click suspicious links, open unexpected attachments, reply with sensitive information, or approve an MFA prompt you did not initiate. Report the message using the company mail client's Report Phishing option, if available, or forward it to the IT Service Desk according to the company's approved reporting process.

## If you clicked a link
Report the incident promptly to the IT Service Desk. If you entered your password, tell IT that credentials may be exposed and follow their instructions to secure the account. Do not delete evidence unless IT instructs you to do so.

## Important limitation
This guide does not define a real reporting email address. Use the contact details configured in your organization's approved directory.
