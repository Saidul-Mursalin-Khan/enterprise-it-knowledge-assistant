# New Employee IT Onboarding Checklist

**Document ID:** IT-009  
**Owner:** IT Support  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Demo policy — fictional company data

## Manager and HR coordination
The manager or authorized onboarding coordinator should submit an access request through the company's approved process. Access must match the employee's role and approved business needs.

## IT setup checklist
1. Confirm the employee identity and start date through the approved onboarding process.
2. Prepare an assigned company device where applicable.
3. Provision the company account and required role-based access after authorization.
4. Ensure MFA is registered for protected systems.
5. Confirm the employee can access required approved applications.
6. Share links to security training and the IT Service Desk process.

## Least privilege
Do not grant broad administrator access by default. Additional permissions require the appropriate approval.

## Missing access
The employee or manager should open a ticket naming the application, required business task, and manager approver. Never use another employee's credentials to work around missing access.
