# Company Data Handling and Classification

**Document ID:** IT-010  
**Owner:** Information Security  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Demo policy — fictional company data

## Demo classifications
- **Public:** Information approved for public release.
- **Internal:** Routine company information intended for employees and approved contractors.
- **Confidential:** Non-public business, customer, employee, financial, or technical information requiring restricted access.
- **Restricted:** Highly sensitive information requiring specifically authorized access and approved handling controls.

## Handling requirements
Share information only with people who have a legitimate business need and the appropriate authorization. Use company-approved storage and collaboration services. Verify recipients before sending confidential material.

## Prohibited handling
Do not upload confidential or restricted company data to personal cloud drives, unapproved AI tools, or public file-sharing services. Do not paste passwords, API keys, access tokens, or private keys into documents or chat.

## Unclear classification
If the correct classification or sharing method is unclear, do not share the information yet. Ask Information Security or the data owner for guidance.

## Scope note
These labels and rules are synthetic examples for a demo and must be replaced with the real company's approved classification scheme before production use.
