# Password and Account Security Policy

**Document ID:** IT-002  
**Owner:** Identity and Access  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Demo policy — fictional company data

## Password requirements
Company passwords must contain at least 14 characters. Employees should use a unique password and must not share it with colleagues or IT support staff.

## Password resets
Use the self-service identity portal for routine password resets. If self-service recovery fails, contact the IT Service Desk for assistance.

## Account lockouts
After five failed login attempts, an account may be temporarily locked. If an account is locked, stop repeated login attempts and follow the self-service recovery process or contact the IT Service Desk.

## Security rule
IT support personnel must never ask an employee to disclose a password or MFA code. Employees must not send these secrets in email, chat, or support tickets.
