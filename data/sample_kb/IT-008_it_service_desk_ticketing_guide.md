# IT Service Desk Ticketing Guide

**Document ID:** IT-008  
**Owner:** IT Support  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Demo policy — fictional company data

## When to create a ticket
Create an IT Service Desk ticket when standard troubleshooting does not resolve a problem, an approved application fails to install, access recovery fails, or a security incident needs reporting.

## Include in a ticket
- A concise description of the problem.
- The affected application or device.
- The approximate time the problem began.
- The exact error message or a screenshot, if safe to share.
- Troubleshooting steps already attempted.

## Never include
Do not include passwords, MFA codes, recovery codes, private keys, or other authentication secrets in a ticket.

## Priority guidance for this demo
- **P1 — Critical:** A confirmed widespread outage affecting a business-critical service.
- **P2 — High:** A major service is unavailable to a team or a security incident may be active.
- **P3 — Normal:** One employee has a problem with a non-critical service and a workaround may exist.
- **P4 — Low:** General questions or routine software requests.

These are illustrative demo definitions only. They do not promise response or resolution times. If uncertain, submit the ticket and describe the business impact accurately.
