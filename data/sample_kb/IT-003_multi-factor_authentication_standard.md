# Multi-Factor Authentication Standard

**Document ID:** IT-003  
**Owner:** Identity and Access  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Demo policy — fictional company data

## Scope
MFA is mandatory for company email, VPN, cloud administration, and other systems designated as protected.

## Approved methods
The approved primary methods are the company authenticator app and registered hardware security keys. SMS may be used only when it has explicitly been enabled as a recovery method.

## Lost device or unavailable factor
If an employee cannot access their registered authenticator or security key, contact the IT Service Desk and follow the identity verification process. Do not borrow another employee's factor or share a one-time code.

## Suspicious prompts
Do not approve an MFA prompt that you did not initiate. Report unexpected prompts to the IT Service Desk promptly.
