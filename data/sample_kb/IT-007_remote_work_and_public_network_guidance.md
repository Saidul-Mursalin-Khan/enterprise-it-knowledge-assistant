# Remote Work and Public Network Guidance

**Document ID:** IT-007  
**Owner:** Workplace Technology  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Demo policy — fictional company data

## Remote access
When working outside the office, connect to the company VPN before accessing internal systems, as required by IT-001.

## Network safety
Prefer trusted, password-protected networks. Avoid conducting company work over unknown or untrusted public Wi-Fi when a safer connection is available. Do not bypass VPN or endpoint-security requirements for convenience.

## Device handling
Keep company devices with you or secured when travelling. Lock the screen when stepping away, and do not allow family members or other people to use a company account.

## Questions
For exceptions or role-specific remote-work requirements, ask the manager and IT Service Desk. This demo document does not establish eligibility for remote work or define employment terms.
